3. Key Recommendations for Domain & Hosting Setup

  • Domain Purchase: Buy the domain on GoDaddy or Namecheap under a dedicated business account. Uncheck all GoDaddy up-sells (no email hosting, no website builder, no paid SSL).
  • Zero-Cost Vercel Hosting: Connect the GitHub repository directly to Vercel. Point GoDaddy's DNS A Record (76.76.21.21) and CNAME to Vercel. You will never receive a hosting invoice.
